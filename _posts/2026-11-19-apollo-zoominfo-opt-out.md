The caller knows your old job title. They want to talk about renewing software you haven’t touched since you left the company. Somehow they’re asking on your personal phone.

An old entry in a business-contact directory could be where they found you. Start with the profile you can identify. If you can see it publicly, save the URL and note the former employer and the phone number or email the directory shows. Those details help when your name matches several people or the email on the record belongs to a job you left.

## Use the directory’s removal form

For **Apollo.io**, open its Privacy Center and choose **Submit opt-out request**. Apollo says it removes the profile from its products and keeps enough information on a suppression list to prevent the profile from being restored. If the form sends you back to an inbox you can no longer use, Apollo also accepts privacy requests at the address in its privacy policy. Write from an address you control and explain that the work inbox is closed to you. [Apollo Privacy Center](https://www.apollo.io/company/privacy-center) · [Apollo privacy policy](https://www.apollo.io/privacy-policy)

For **ZoomInfo**, start on **Request to Remove Your Information from ZoomInfo**. The form begins with an email address so the company can locate your information. Follow the prompts and save the request notification. If verification depends on an inbox you’ve lost access to, use the contact route on the form to ask how it can match the old record. Include the company and role shown on the old listing so the privacy team has something to match. [ZoomInfo’s removal form](https://privacyrequest.zoominfo.com/remove/verify)

## If the next sales email still turns up

A salesperson might have saved your details to their own contact system months ago. Apollo tells its customers that a removal from Apollo’s database can leave copies in saved contacts, sales sequences and external systems. It provides a removal list that customers can use to find those records. [Apollo’s guidance for customers who saved a contact](https://knowledge.apollo.io/hc/en-us/articles/19331318468621-Understand-Removal-Requests-and-Stay-Compliant-on-Apollo)

If a company keeps reaching out, ask its privacy contact to remove the record it uses to call or email you. You can keep the request short.

> I left [former company] and I’m still receiving outreach about that role. Please remove my personal phone number and the former work email from the contact record you use for outreach. Can you confirm when you’ve updated it?

Save any answer that names the source. If the same outdated job title appears in another directory, you have a specific record to check. If the number also turns up in public listings with no connection to your job, handle those URLs separately.
