## 1. Choose the plan for your work

**Agent at CAD $4.99/month is the starting point for automatic protection.** It includes the recurring protection workflows, rather than requiring you to ask for every check in chat. Security and Max keep those protection features with larger allowances. Chat is for on-demand help.

| Plan | Monthly price | Best starting point | Scheduled protection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Agent** | **CAD $4.99** | Everyday automatic protection, broker removal, monitoring, private cases and updates | Included |
| Chat | CAD $2.99 | Questions and on-demand investigations in dashboard chat | Requires Agent or another protection plan |
| Security | CAD $13.37 | Professional security research and MCP/API delegation with more capacity | Included |
| Max | CAD $35 | Agent protection with larger allowances for ongoing work | Included |

Prices are billed monthly. Taxes may apply. Account creation is free; paid features start after payment is confirmed. Cancel future renewals in Billing.

### What each subscription includes

- **Chat:** up to 20 messages per UTC day and 2 saved chats; 350 AI credits and 100 hosted-compute minutes per month. It includes supported on-demand chat tools. Scheduled monitoring, broker campaigns, service email alerts and MCP/API access require a protection plan.
- **Agent:** automatic protection profile preparation; eligible broker campaigns and follow-up; enabled-source identity and dark web exposure monitoring; private scam and phishing cases; up to 3 websites, 10 identity targets and 3 saved chats; supported DNS setup, email updates, MCP and API access. Monthly personal allowances are 40 AI credits, 40 hosted-compute minutes and 20,000 MCP requests.
- **Security:** Agent protection features with up to 15 websites, 50 identity targets and 15 saved chats. Monthly allowances are 500 AI credits, 1,000 hosted-compute minutes and 100,000 MCP requests.
- **Max:** Agent protection features with up to 30 websites, 100 identity targets and 30 saved chats. Monthly allowances are 3,000 AI credits, 4,000 hosted-compute minutes and 500,000 MCP requests.

Broker processing and service emails have separate monthly budgets. Broker work does not spend your personal AI or compute credits. Initial campaigns progress in batches and may span monthly cycles. Advanced tooling requires its own reviewed approval, regardless of which subscription you choose.

[Compare plans and allowances in Help](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/help#help-plans).

## 2. Sign in and review automatic protection

1. Open [Agent Garrett](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/) and choose **Sign in**. Use Google, Facebook or email. Email sign-in uses the code or link sent to your inbox.
2. Review the plan, included features and payment total before checkout. The dashboard confirms access after payment is confirmed.
3. Open **Dashboard**. Garrett prepares your protection profile automatically from the information available in your account. Review what is being monitored, correct details and complete any missing setup.
4. Look at **What needs your attention**, the latest observations and the next scheduled checks.

Garrett hosts the agent backend. Your browser and computer can be closed while enabled cloud work follows its schedule. Connected accounts, devices and recovery modes can need additional setup. A saved profile does not mean that every account or device is connected.

## 3. Remove your data from data brokers

1. Open **Data removal**. Your protection subscription enrolls you in the supported removal workflow.
2. Review your saved name and contact details. Choose where you live so requests use the appropriate route. Add optional matching information only when it belongs to you or someone you are authorized to represent.
3. Read **Removal permission**. Review the approved fields, search choices and automatic-removal setting before signing. The signature must be yours.
4. Follow the campaign, individual requests and **Campaign completion report**. Complete direct verification when a broker requires it.

Request counts and removal counts are different. A prepared request, a submission, a broker reply and an observed absent listing are separate stages. Garrett tracks the work and eligible follow-up. Pause scheduled work or revoke authorization from its controls when needed.

[Watch the short removal guide](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/help#help-video-02-data-removal).

## 4. Investigate a suspicious message or scam

1. Open **Message checks** and choose the starting point that fits: a suspicious message, something you clicked or shared, or a payment you already made.
2. Describe what happened. Paste the message or attach supported evidence. Leave out passwords, sign-in codes, card details and unrelated personal information.
3. Start the automatic check. Garrett chooses supported checks and keeps the assessment, indicators, evidence and recovery steps in one private case.
4. Add follow-up details to the same case. Review scheduled follow-ups and the steps that require your action.

Account changes, payments and external reports remain actions for the account owner to review and complete. Use trusted contact details and the official service when recovering access. If you already sent money, contact the payment provider promptly.

[Watch the message-check guide](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/help#help-video-03-message-checks).

## 5. Review identity and dark web exposure

Open **Online identity**. Review the identifiers being monitored, the latest source observation and its timestamp. Use the target's evidence view to inspect findings, attribution and activity.

To add another target, open the monitoring controls, choose its type and complete the ownership or authorization step. Public leak-post searches have separate controls because the identifier goes to that provider.

A mention is a lead to review, not proof that an account was taken over or that a particular person was responsible. Read the source coverage and any missing checks. Use the monitor's own pause control when you want to stop it.

[Watch the exposure guide](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/help#help-video-04-online-identity).

## 6. Add a website to Website Watch

Open **Websites** and add the public address of a site you own or manage. Choose the behavior you need:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Watch** | Checks public website signals such as availability, expected text, public DNS and security headers. |
| **Investigate** | Adds bounded evidence gathering and a recorded case history when something needs attention. |
| **Handle** | Adds supported recovery for an eligible connected website Worker, using a healthy version you explicitly approved. |

Review the saved target, latest result, observation time and next scheduled check. An incomplete check is a gap to review. Optional recovery needs its own connection and approval; choosing a mode does not authorize arbitrary site changes.

[Watch the website guide](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/help#help-video-05-websites).

## 7. Ask questions, manage updates and billing

Use **Chat** to describe a concern in plain English. Keep related follow-up questions in the same saved conversation, and inspect the evidence behind the answer.

In **Email updates**, review the address, confirmation state and notification choices. Send a test when available, then check your inbox and spam folder. An accepted send receipt does not prove inbox delivery.

Open **Plan & settings** for subscription, sign-in and billing controls. Each monitor or case has its own pause control. Cancelling future renewals in Billing is a separate action.

[Watch the chat, updates and billing guide](https://agent.garrettstimpson.ca/help#help-video-06-chat-alerts-billing).
