Choose a plan and get started

Start with the plan that fits the help you need. Chat is for on-demand questions and investigations. Agent, at four dollars and ninety-nine cents Canadian a month, adds scheduled protection, data-broker removal, monitoring and email updates. Security and Max keep the protection features with larger allowances. Sign in with Google, Facebook or email, then review your plan before checkout. After payment is confirmed, open your dashboard. Garrett prepares your protection profile automatically from the information available in your account. Review what is monitored and anything that needs your attention.

Remove your data from data brokers

Open Data removal from your dashboard. Review your removal profile and add the details needed to identify your listings. Check the authorization before starting broker requests. Your profile helps Garrett find supported listings and prepare the right removal work. Follow the campaign and individual requests to see what was prepared, what was sent, and whether a broker needs a step from you. A sent request and a confirmed removal are different stages. Return here for progress and follow-up. Use the pause controls when you want to stop scheduled work.

Investigate a suspicious message

Choose Message checks when an email, text or link looks suspicious. Start a private case and paste the message, or attach supported evidence. Leave out passwords, payment details and unrelated personal information. Start the investigation, then read the recorded indicators, risk assessment and suggested next steps. You can add context to the same case and follow the work without starting over. Automated follow-ups stay with the case, within your plan allowance. Changes to accounts, payments and external reports are decisions you make yourself. If money or account access is at risk, begin the relevant recovery steps promptly.

Review dark web and identity exposure

Open Online identity to review the identifiers Garrett monitors. Add an email address or identity you own or are authorized to represent, and complete any required ownership step. Read the latest observation, its timestamp and the sources that answered. A breach-source mention is a lead to review, not proof that an account was taken over. Open the evidence and check what information was exposed before choosing your response. Keep important account passwords unique and turn on stronger sign-in protection where available. You can pause a monitor and return later to review its schedule.

Set up Website Watch

Open Websites and add the public address of a site you own or manage. Watch checks public website signals. Investigate adds bounded evidence gathering when something needs attention. Handle adds supported recovery for an eligible connected website Worker, using a healthy version you explicitly approved. Start with the mode you need and review the saved target. Check the latest result, recorded time and next scheduled check. Open the evidence when a result needs attention. An incomplete check is a gap to review. Cloud checks continue with your browser closed, within the configured schedule and plan allowance.

Ask questions, manage updates and billing

Open Chat to ask Garrett about a concern in plain English. Keep follow-up questions in the same saved conversation and inspect the evidence behind the answer. In Email updates, review your address, confirmation status and notification preferences. Send a test when available, then check your inbox and spam folder. An accepted send is not proof of inbox delivery. Open Plan and settings to review your subscription, usage and billing controls. Each monitor or case has its own pause control. Cancelling future renewals is managed in Billing. Return to Help for plan details and the walkthrough for the task you want to complete.
